## Overview

An interactive wrap-up after participants have had time to use agents in their
own work. Your experiences are the starting point: what you built or tried,
where agents were useful, where they failed, the problems you ran into, and the
techniques you developed to deal with them.

## What we will cover

- **Your experiences.** Short demonstrations and stories from participants.
- **Best practices.** What we can generalise from those experiences.
- **Open questions and next steps.** Potential directions for projects or
  further research.
- **GPU compute.** Our own experience applying for GPU compute through SURF and
  EuroHPC: what the application process involves and what we learned.

## Interactive component

Participant demonstrations, experience sharing, troubleshooting, our
experiences with SURF and EuroHPC compute applications, and open discussion.

## How to prepare

You do not need a polished project. Something you tried that failed in an
interesting way is just as useful to the group as something that worked. If
you would like to show something, a few minutes and a couple of screenshots or
a live demo is plenty.
