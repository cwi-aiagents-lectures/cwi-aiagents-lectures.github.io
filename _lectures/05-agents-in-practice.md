## Overview

An interactive wrap-up after participants have had time to use agents in their
own work. Your experiences and agent journals are the starting point: what you
built or tried, where agents were useful, where they failed, the problems you
ran into, and the techniques you developed to deal with them. We consolidate
what the group has learned and give pointers for continuing after the course.

## What we will cover

- **Your experiences.** Short demonstrations and stories from participants,
  about five minutes each. Failures are welcome.
- **Best practices.** Drawn live from the demos and journals into a shared
  one-page guide.
- **GPU compute.** Our experience applying for compute through SURF (Snellius)
  and EuroHPC: what the process involves, and what the compute is good for.
  That includes running open-weight models and agents on your own
  infrastructure for sensitive data, and fine-tuning.
- **Open discussion.** How academic work is changing, open questions, and
  ideas for projects.

## Interactive component

Participant demonstrations, experience sharing, troubleshooting, our
experience with SURF and EuroHPC compute applications, and open discussion.

## How to prepare

You do not need a polished project. Something you tried that failed in an
interesting way is just as useful to the group as something that worked. If
you would like to show something, a few minutes and a couple of screenshots or
a live demo is plenty. Your agent journal is a good place to start.
