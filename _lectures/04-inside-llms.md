## Overview

A technical overview for participants who want to understand what happens
behind the interface. It is designed to stay accessible without a machine
learning background, and to be a starting point for anyone considering
research or more technical work with LLMs and agents.

## What we will cover

- **Pre-training.** How a base language model is produced.
- **Supervised fine-tuning (SFT).** How a base model is turned into an
  assistant.
- **Reinforcement learning (RL).** How models are improved further, and how
  agentic models in particular can be trained.
- **AI safety.** Where safety and alignment problems arise in the development
  and deployment of increasingly capable models and agents.
- **Where to go next.** Resources for participants interested in pursuing LLM
  or agent research.

## Interactive component

Discussion and exercises that connect the agent behaviour you have seen in
the first three sessions to the underlying model and its training process.
