## Overview

This session moves from individual interactions to agentic *workflows*. By the
end of it, you should have the practical foundation needed to use AI agents
independently and effectively.

## What we will cover

- **Approaching larger tasks.** Decomposing a problem into pieces an agent
  can handle, and deciding which pieces you should keep for yourself.
- **Managing longer interactions.** Keeping a long piece of work coherent
  over many steps.
- **Inspecting and verifying.** Common failure modes, and how to check
  agent behaviour and outputs instead of taking them on trust.
- **Agents in research.** Practical approaches to literature review, getting
  into a new research area, organising and comparing papers, checking claims
  and references, and supporting scientific writing, while keeping human
  verification and responsibility for the final work.
- **Agents in technical work.** Programming, debugging, data analysis, and
  automating multi-step tasks.

## Interactive component
